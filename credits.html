Tennis Paradise concept: credits and notes

This is an illustrative StoryFrame concept prepared for the ATP Tour. It is not an official ATP, BNP Paribas Open or Indian Wells Tennis Garden experience, and it implies no endorsement.

Images

The 360-degree stadium scene and every story picture are AI-generated concept images, made for this demonstration. They are not photographs of the Indian Wells Tennis Garden, and the people in them are not real. No logos or player likenesses are used.

Sound

The crowd, rally and applause ambience is synthesized for this concept. Wind, birdsong and the gathering tones are synthesized in the browser.

Type

Space Grotesk, used under the SIL Open Font License. StoryFrame supplied its own branding.

Facts

The stories linked from this experience were fact checked in November 2026; their sources are listed in the content outline. The quiz and the timing challenge use facts from Wikipedia (tennis scoring), Merriam-Webster (deuce) and Tennis.com (return reaction time).

Measurement

Page views, interactions and outbound clicks are recorded as anonymous events (no personal data) for demonstration of StoryFrame reporting. Any dashboard figures shown alongside this concept are sample data.
